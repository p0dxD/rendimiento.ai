# Code of conduct

rendimiento follows the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/). In short: be kind and patient, assume good intent, welcome people whatever their experience, language or background (issues and pull requests in English or Spanish are both fine), and keep criticism about the work, never the person.

Harassment, insults, or publishing someone's private information are not accepted, here or in any space of the project. The maintainers may remove comments, code or contributions that break this code, and may ban, for a while or for good, whoever breaks it.

To report a problem, write privately to a maintainer of the [rendimiento-ai](https://github.com/rendimiento-ai) organization. Reports are kept confidential.
