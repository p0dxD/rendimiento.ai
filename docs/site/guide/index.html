    Using it¶

    How to use rendimiento's features, one chapter per area:

    • rendimiento.yaml reference: every field, with defaults and examples.
    • Apps day to day: onboarding, migrating existing apps, domains, secrets, volumes, jobs, GPUs, rollbacks, deleting.
    • Builds and tests: Dockerfile or Railpack, tests, what gets rebuilt, and when.
    • Tasks: commands in CI: mobile builds, smoke tests and scripts as CI steps, with secrets.
    • Reliability: uptime checks of every service, charted: uptime, response times, outages, releases.
    • Visits: visitors, page views and top pages from Umami, on every app and on the dashboard.
    • Notifications: emails about failed builds, rolled-back releases, outages and recoveries.
    • La Ruta: the platform's memory, for people and AI agents: decisions, manuals, to-dos, handovers and lived stories, reachable over MCP.
    • Needs and the services catalog: databases, caches and other services, wired in without configuration.
    • The Universe: every app from above, as a village: barrios of apps, the roads between them with their calls and latency, and each app's services inside.
    • Libraries: rendimiento for Node, Python and Java: the business moments, events and flags on the traces.
    • Add-ons: installing and adopting cluster software from Helm charts and git.
    • Renovate: dependency updates.
