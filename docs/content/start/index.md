# Start here

Three short chapters give you the whole picture before any detail:

1. **[Why rendimiento exists](vision.md)**: the problem it replaced, the decisions that shaped it, and the history of how your apps moved onto it.
2. **[Concepts and vocabulary](concepts.md)**: the dozen words the rest of the book uses (app, service, run, release, add-on, need…) and how they relate.
3. **[A tour of the UI](tour.md)**: every page of the dashboard and what it is for.

After that, pick the part of the book that matches what you want to do: run it ([Your environment](../environment/index.md)), understand it ([Architecture](../architecture/index.md)), use it ([Using it](../guide/index.md)), change it ([Developing it](../develop/index.md)) or grow it ([Growing it](../future/index.md)).
